7 signs your home needs better insulation

By the Armoseal Insulation team

The short answer

If your energy bills are climbing, some rooms are much hotter than others, or your AC seems to run all day, your insulation may be thin, old or missing in places.

Insulation is out of sight, so it's easy to forget about. But it plays a big part in how comfortable your home feels and how much you pay to cool it. Here are some common signs it needs attention:

  1. Your energy bills keep going up. If you haven't changed your habits but your bills have crept higher, heat may be getting in more easily.
  2. Some rooms are always hotter or colder. Uneven temperatures from room to room often point to gaps in insulation.
  3. Your AC runs almost nonstop. When cool air escapes and hot air sneaks in, your system has to work harder to keep up.
  4. The upstairs is much warmer than downstairs. Heat from the attic can pour into upper rooms if the attic isn't well insulated.
  5. You feel drafts. Air moving around windows, doors or outlets means there are leaks that could be sealed.
  6. The insulation in your attic looks thin or flattened. If you can easily see the wooden beams (joists) on your attic floor, you likely don't have enough.
  7. You see signs of moisture or pests. Wet, stained or damaged insulation doesn't work as well and may need to be replaced.

What to do next

If any of these sound familiar, it doesn't necessarily mean you need a big project. Sometimes a small fix makes a big difference. A quick home inspection will tell you exactly where you stand.
