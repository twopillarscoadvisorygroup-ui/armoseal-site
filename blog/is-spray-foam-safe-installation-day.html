Is spray foam safe? What to expect on installation day

By the Armoseal Insulation team

The short answer

Once spray foam has fully cured, it's a stable, long-lasting material. While it's being sprayed and curing, people and pets need to stay out of the area for a period of time, usually about a day. We'll tell you exactly how long for your job.

Before the job

We'll walk you through the plan ahead of time: which areas we're insulating, how long it will take and when you'll need to be out of the space. No surprises.

  • We'll let you know what to move or cover
  • We'll confirm the times you and your pets should be away
  • We'll answer any questions you have

During installation

While spray foam is being applied, it gives off fumes, so our crew wears protective gear and the area is ventilated. This is why people and pets need to stay away from the work area while we're spraying and while the foam cures.

Most manufacturers recommend staying out for about 24 hours, though the time can vary by product. We'll give you the exact time for your job and stick to it.

After installation

Once it has cured, spray foam becomes a solid, stable material that stays in place for years. We'll clean up the work area and walk you through what we did before we leave.

Have a specific concern?

If anyone in your home has asthma, allergies or chemical sensitivities, please tell us ahead of time. We'll talk through your options honestly. Sometimes a different type of insulation is the better fit.
