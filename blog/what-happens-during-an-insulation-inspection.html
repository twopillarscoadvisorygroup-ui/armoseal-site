What happens during a home insulation inspection?

By the Armoseal Insulation team

The short answer

We look at your attic and other key areas, check how much insulation you have and its condition, look for air leaks and moisture, and then explain what we found in plain language, with honest recommendations.

What we look at

  • Your attic: how much insulation is there, what type it is and what shape it's in
  • Gaps and air leaks: places where outside air may be getting in
  • Moisture or damage: wet, stained or pest-damaged insulation that isn't doing its job
  • Problem rooms: any rooms you've noticed are too hot, too cold or drafty

What you'll get

When we're done, we'll sit down with you and explain what we found. We'll tell you:

  • What's working fine and can be left alone
  • What could be improved, and why
  • Your options, with clear prices for each

If your home doesn't need any work, we'll tell you that too. We'd rather earn your trust than sell you something you don't need.

How to prepare

Not much! Just make sure we can get to your attic access, which is often a hatch in a closet, hallway or garage ceiling. If you've noticed any problem rooms or high bills, jot them down so we can take a closer look.
