Can better insulation really lower my energy bill?

By the Armoseal Insulation team

The short answer

Yes, for many homes. ENERGY STAR estimates that homeowners can save an average of about 15% on heating and cooling costs by sealing air leaks and adding insulation.

Where your cooling money goes

In Florida, air conditioning is one of the biggest parts of most energy bills. Every bit of cool air that leaks out, and every bit of heat that soaks in through the attic, means your AC has to run longer.

Insulation plus air sealing

Insulation slows heat down. Air sealing closes the gaps and cracks where air slips in and out. Doing both together gives you the best results. According to ENERGY STAR, sealing and insulating can save homeowners an average of about 15% on heating and cooling costs.

Spray foam does both jobs at once. With other types of insulation, gaps are usually sealed as a separate step.

More than just savings

Lower bills are nice, but most of our customers tell us the biggest difference is how their home feels:

  • Rooms stay a more even temperature
  • The upstairs isn't so hot in summer
  • The AC doesn't run as often
  • The house is quieter

Every home is different

How much you'll save depends on your home, how much insulation you have now and how you use your AC. We'll give you an honest idea of what to expect before you decide anything.
