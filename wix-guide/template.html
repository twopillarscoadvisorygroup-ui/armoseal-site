Step 1 Before you start

  1. Work on your existing Wix site, the one connected to armosealinsulation.com. That way your 2-year plan and your domain stay attached, and nothing needs to be moved.
  2. Make a backup copy first. In your Wix dashboard, open the site’s Site Actions (⋯) menu and choose Duplicate Site. If anything goes wrong, the old site is still there.
  3. Open the Editor and go through the old pages. You will rebuild Home, and create Services, About Us, Blog and Contact. The old Portfolio page can be deleted. Book Online is optional: the new design doesn’t include it, so delete it unless you still want online booking.
  4. Keep the new site open in another tab to look at while you work: http://localhost:8080 (it works while the preview is running on this computer).

Wix sometimes renames its menus. If a button name in this guide doesn’t match exactly, look for the closest match. Tick Done on each step as you finish. This browser remembers your ticks.

Step 11 Check the phone view

Many visitors will be on phones. Click the phone icon at the top of the Wix Editor to switch to the mobile view, then go through every page:

  • Columns should stack: picture on top, text underneath. Cards should go one per row.
  • Text should be at least 18px. Wix sometimes shrinks it on phones, so make it bigger if needed.
  • Buttons should be big and easy to tap. Make the main buttons stretch the full width.
  • Add a “Call” button that’s always visible. In the mobile editor, look for Mobile Tools → Quick Action Bar and turn on a Call action with %%phone%%. If your editor doesn’t have it, add a “Call Us” button and set it to Pin to Screen at the bottom.
  • Top bar: on phones, show only “Call us: %%phone%%”, centered. Hide the service-area text there.

Step 12 Clean up and publish

Your current Wix site still has some leftover template content. Make sure all of it is gone:

  • The placeholder email info@mysite.com in the footer. The real email is %%email%%.
  • Social media icons that link to Wix’s own Facebook, Instagram, X and TikTok pages. Remove them, or link them to Armoseal’s real profiles.
  • “© 2035” in the footer. It should say 2026.
  • Old pages you aren’t using: Welcome, Portfolio, and Book Online if you don’t want it.

Then:

  1. In the dashboard, open Settings → Business Info and enter the phone, email, address (400 N Tampa St, Tampa, FL 33602) and hours (Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
  2. Click Preview and go through every page and button. Tap every phone link on a real phone.
  3. Send yourself a test quote request through the Contact form and make sure it arrives.
  4. Click Publish. Then open armosealinsulation.com on a computer and on a phone.
